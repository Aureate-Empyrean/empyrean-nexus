# Branding and project status

The software is AGPL-3.0-or-later. The Aureate Empyrean name, visual identity, logos and assertion of official-project status are separate from that software license. A detailed trademark policy and any registrations are future work; this document does not claim registered rights or create a new software-license restriction.

Forks may describe their origin truthfully and retain legally required attribution. They must not falsely imply that they are published, reviewed or endorsed by Aureate Empyrean. Encourage useful changes upstream without portraying independent forks as prohibited.

Current identity: restrained dark celestial neutrals, Aureate Gold as an accent, the Nexus mark (a star held in a ring of connected points), Roboto for interface text and, optionally, Greater Theory for the fixed wordmark when it is installed locally (it is not distributed with Nexus). The mark is still placeholder branding. Interface behavior, accessibility and clear trust information take precedence over decoration.

Official means organization-published; Verified means reviewed through an established process; Community means independently maintained or unverified in this installation. These are provenance/review descriptions, not moral judgments or security guarantees. V1 does not verify provenance and therefore assigns Community to all manually supplied manifests, including the local reference.
